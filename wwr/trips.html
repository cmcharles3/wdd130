Family Splash Run

Difficulty: Class I - II (Beginner)

Perfect for families, first-timers, and young children. Enjoy gentle currents, scenic mountain backdrops, and plenty of opportunities to swim in calm pools along the route.

  • Duration: Half Day (3 Hours)
  • Min Age: 6 years old
  • Includes: Lunch, safety gear, guide

Canyon Challenge

Difficulty: Class III - IV (Intermediate)

Our most popular trip! Tackle continuous technical rapids, steep drops, and exhilarating waves carved through narrow granite canyons. High energy from start to finish.

  • Duration: Full Day (6 Hours)
  • Min Age: 12 years old
  • Includes: Riverside BBQ lunch, gear, transportation

Extreme Thunder Gorge

Difficulty: Class IV - V (Advanced)

Designed for experienced rafters seeking maximum adrenaline. Paddle through extreme hydraulics, massive standing waves, and technical chutes under expert instruction.

  • Duration: 2 Days (Overnight Camp)
  • Min Age: 18 years old
  • Includes: All meals, camping equipment, expert guide
